# Extract typed causal coaching facts instead of prose policy

Game Review comments need to lead with the relevant board feature, show the tactical mechanism, and calibrate severity without letting the language layer invent facts. At the time of this decision, the Rule Extractor emitted three deterministic fact families per Critical Moment under `teaching-facts/v2`; the pre-production strict-v1 reset later superseded that label with `teaching-facts/v1`. Played-Move Effects are board-derived observations from a small allowlist: captured piece, advanced passed pawn, newly attacked higher-value or undefended piece, and allowed queen exchange. Residual Outcome classifies the mover's standing before and after the move. Winning starts at +300 centipawns or forced mate, favorable starts at +100, balanced is above -100, unfavorable is above -300, and losing covers the rest. Its classifications are missed forced mate, advantage kept, standing kept, advantage reduced, advantage lost, and now worse. Centipawn scores do not establish that one win is faster than another, so the model does not claim this. Prose may say the Player is "still winning" only when `standingAfter` is `winning`. Tactical Mechanism replays the returned best line and truncates it at a verified payoff: mate, promotion, a net gain of at least two pawn units, or a queen exchange. It also marks the mover's first forcing move. A mate payoff requires the returned line to reach checkmate.

All three families live in `causal_facts.rs`. The module parses each trusted imported position and played move once, and Rule Extraction fails if that internal invariant is broken. Invalid principal variations also fail instead of silently omitting the mechanism. Full Game Review Critical Moments always have a Residual Outcome. Only Player-Selected terminal facts may omit one.

Trapped-piece and loose-piece detection were considered and dropped because neither has a deterministic predicate. A material-winning line alone does not prove a loose piece, fork, or skewer, so `practice-lessons/v2` does not make The Fork or The Skewer eligible from that evidence. Passed-pawn promotion maps to the reviewed Key Squares lesson. Queen exchange has no honest lesson in the allowlist and supports none. Move intent stays out of the initial review; the Review Session intent workflow remains the only channel for purpose claims. `validate-review` stays structural. Semantic grounding continues to rely on the coach skill's writing rules over these typed facts.
