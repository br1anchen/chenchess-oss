Small-world opening study

A ChenChess research spike. One opening, one tray, five stages — you build the world before you play in it.

What this prototype is arguing. Nothing here is a line to recall. Stage 1 makes you rebuild the position from its ideas, stage 2 asks for the plan in your own words, stage 3 for the break the structure wants, and stage 4 takes you off book around move seven — where the measured data says a club player actually ends up. The session keeps no due dates and no durable deck: the tray is the container, and you demolish it at the end.
