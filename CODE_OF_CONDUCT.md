# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to build something good.

## The longer version

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, which is reproduced in full at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In this space that means, concretely:

- Criticise the work, not the person. "This drops a case" is useful; "this is
  sloppy" is not.
- Harassment, in public or private, ends participation. So do sexualised
  language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and publishing someone
  else's private information.
- Disagreement is expected and welcome. Disagreement carried on after a
  decision is made is not, unless you have new evidence.
- Chess is a game people are often insecure about. Nobody's rating is a
  reason to take them less seriously here.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies wherever someone is representing the project.

Reports go to the maintainer through
[github.com/br1anchen](https://github.com/br1anchen). Reports are handled
privately. Because this repository is a published snapshot rather than an
active development forum, the practical remedies are limited — blocking, and
removing contributed content — and they will be used where they apply.
